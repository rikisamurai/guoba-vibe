Two clocks, one pipeline

The network has its own rhythm; the UI should have another. Deltas land in a raw buffer immediately, while a scheduler decides how much becomes visibleText per frame.

  • Completed blocks are frozen with stable ids — they never re-render.
  • Only the dirty tail is re-parsed on each commit.
  • Heavy nodes get their own clock entirely.
